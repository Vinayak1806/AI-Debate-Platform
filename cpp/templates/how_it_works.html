How AI Debate Teams Work

An elegant approach to mastering the art of persuasion.

1

Choose Your Topic

Browse our extensive collection of debate topics or use our search feature to find topics that interest you.

2

Select Position

Choose whether you want to argue for or against the topic. The AI will strategically take the opposing position.

3

Launch Opening

Begin with your opening argument. The AI will analyze your points and respond with structured counterarguments.

4

Engage Intelligently

Continue the conversation by responding. Use evidence, logic, and persuasive techniques to strengthen your case.

5

Receive Feedback

Unlock achievements and gain xp on your argumentation quality, logical consistency, and overall debating skills.

6

Track Progress

View your debate history, win/loss rates, and skill improvement over time via your premium profile dashboard.

A Glimpse into the Arena

You I believe that artificial intelligence will create more jobs than it destroys, driving a new renaissance of tools.
AI Antagonist While AI may create roles, historical precedents show that technological advancements often lead to net job loss in short-term. Automation in manufacturing rapidly displaced millions of manual laborers before equilibrium was reached.
You But those workers were retrained for new industries. AI will be similar - it will eliminate routine drudgery but create an explosive demand for creative, emotional, and highly technical coordination roles.
AI Antagonist That's a valid point on retraining, but the scale and velocity of AI adoption drastically outpaces our geopolitical ability to retrain massive swaths of workers successfully. How do we close the short-term transition gap?
