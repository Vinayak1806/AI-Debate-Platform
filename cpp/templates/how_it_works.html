How AI Debate & Dialogue Works

1

Choose Your Topic

Browse our extensive collection of debate topics or use our search feature to find topics that interest you. Topics range from current events to philosophical questions.

2

Select Your Position

Choose whether you want to argue for or against the topic. The AI will take the opposing position, creating a balanced debate environment.

3

Start the Debate

Begin with your opening argument. The AI will analyze your points and respond with counterarguments, evidence, and logical reasoning.

4

Engage in Dialogue

Continue the conversation by responding to the AI's arguments. Use evidence, logic, and persuasive techniques to strengthen your position.

5

Receive Feedback

After the debate, get detailed feedback on your argumentation quality, use of evidence, logical consistency, and overall debating skills.

6

Track Progress

View your debate history, win/loss statistics, and skill improvement over time in your personalized profile dashboard.

See It In Action

You: I believe that artificial intelligence will create more jobs than it destroys.
AI: While AI may create some new roles, historical precedents show that technological advancements often lead to net job loss in affected sectors. For example, the industrial revolution and automation in manufacturing displaced millions of workers.
You: But those workers were retrained for new industries. AI will be similar - it will eliminate routine jobs but create demand for creative and technical roles.
AI: That's a valid point about retraining, but the scale and speed of AI adoption may outpace our ability to retrain workers. Additionally, many routine jobs that AI replaces may not have direct creative or technical equivalents.
